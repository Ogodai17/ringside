Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What We Collect

Ringside is a free fight prediction game. We collect minimal data to keep the site running:

  • Local storage data — your balance, picks, and preferences are saved in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Analytics — we use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site (page views, time on site, general location by country). This data is anonymous and aggregated.
  • Cookies — third-party advertising partners (Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

What We Don't Collect

  • We don't collect your name, email, or any personal information
  • We don't require account creation to use the site
  • We don't process any real money or financial information
  • We don't sell or share personal data with third parties

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense — serves advertisements. Google may use cookies to personalize ads based on your browsing history. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — tracks anonymous usage statistics. See Google Analytics Data Practices.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

No Real Gambling

Ringside uses fictional currency only. No real money is wagered, won, or lost. The site is a prediction game for entertainment purposes. There is no financial risk to users.

Children's Privacy

Ringside is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
