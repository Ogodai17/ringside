About Ringside

Free fight predictions. No real money. Just bragging rights.

Ringside is a prediction game for combat sports fans. Call the winner, the method, the round — then watch fight night and see how your picks stack up against the leaderboard.

We started with ONE Championship because it's the most underserved major promotion in the prediction space. ONE runs world-class MMA, Muay Thai, kickboxing, and grappling under one roof, and there's nothing out there that lets fans put their fight IQ to the test across all those disciplines.

How It Works

Pick Your Fights

Choose a winner, method of victory, round, or whether it goes the distance. Every pick has odds.

Fake Money, Real Stakes

You start with 5,000 R (Ringside credits). No real money involved — it's all about the leaderboard.

Daily Top-Up

Go broke? Claim 300 R per day to get back in the game. Everyone gets a second chance.

Live Results

Fight results are pulled automatically. Your bets resolve and your balance updates after each event.

What This Isn't

Ringside is not a sportsbook. No real money is wagered, deposited, or withdrawn. There are no cash prizes. This is a free prediction game built for fans who want to test their fight knowledge without financial risk.

Who Built This

Ringside is an independent project built by a combat sports fan. Fight data is sourced from publicly available event listings. The site is open source on GitHub.
